Jevil Fight Simulator

Play the Jevil boss fight from DELTARUNE Chapter 1 for free, right in your browser, on PC or mobile. No download needed. Fight with Kris, Susie and Ralsei, dodge all of Jevil's attacks with your SOUL, use Pirouette and Hypnosis to tire him out, and survive the Final Chaos. CHAOS, CHAOS!

The battle logic is ported from the original game, so the attacks, damage, TP and ACTs work like the real fight. Turn on Easy Mode on the title screen to take half damage while you practice.

Controls

Z or Enter: confirm   X or Shift: cancel   Arrow keys: move. On phones, use the on-screen pad and buttons.

Practice mode

Choose PRACTICE on the title screen (or open practice mode directly) to dodge Jevil's 16 attacks in order, Bad Time Simulator style. Lose your whole HP bar and the attack restarts; survive it and you move on to the next one, all the way to the Final Chaos. You can start from any attack and pick 90 HP, 45 HP or 1 HP (no hit).

For free practice, sandbox mode repeats any attack forever: press [ and ] to switch attacks and I for invincibility.
